# Benchmark and positioning

## What we benchmarked

Snappy is the reference for corporate gifting where the recipient chooses.
From its public site and product pages, the patterns that matter for us are:

| Pattern | What it means | Our POC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recipient choice | Curated collections at a price point; recipient picks, prices hidden | Budget collections (M2, M4) |
| Pay for what's chosen | Budget held or estimated at send; charged on claim; unclaimed comes back | Hold, true-up, release (M3, M4, M6) |
| Many channels | Email, SMS, Slack, Teams, links | All six channels (M3, M5) |
| Automation | Birthdays, anniversaries, onboarding from HR systems | Milestone worker and one HR system (M5) |
| Team budgets and approvals | Per-team spend and controls | Budget accounts and approvals (M1, M3) |
| Tracking | Gift funnel and delivery status | Dashboard and exports (M6) |
| Integrations and API | Triggered gifting, CRM and HR connectors | Later (public API, webhooks) |

We take the patterns, not the brand, design or content.

## What makes ours different

1. **commercetools-native.** Built on the client's own commerce platform:
   their catalog, tax, orders and reporting, not a closed SaaS.
2. **A merchant can run it.** A brand (like the original "choose your tin"
   client) can run the marketplace with its own products as one vendor,
   and add partner vendors later.
3. **Real orders, real money.** Vendors and finance see true totals; the
   ledger reconciles to commercetools to the cent.
4. **An accelerator.** The practice can stand it up for the next client in
   weeks: setup scripts, seed data, adapters for their providers.

## The demo story (M7)

1. Maya funds her People team with $5,000 (Stripe test mode).
2. She builds "Anything up to $50" from three vendors.
3. A birthday campaign sends itself through Slack; a prospect gift goes by
   email and a printed QR code.
4. Sam opens the gift on a phone, picks a tin of cookies, enters an address.
5. The vendor portal shows the order; the vendor adds tracking; Sam and Maya
   see it shipped.
6. Maya's balance shows the hold released and the real cost captured; the
   ledger reconciles to the commercetools order.

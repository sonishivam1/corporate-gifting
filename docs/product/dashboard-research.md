# Sender dashboard: what other gifting platforms do

Research from 29 Oct 2026. It answers one question: how do corporate gifting
platforms lay out the sender's dashboard, and where does ours stand? It
doesn't settle the B2C workspace and money model. That is still an
[open decision](../business/open-questions.md).

**Sources:** the vendors' public help centres, product pages and developer
docs. No logged-in demos. Sendoso's help pages block automated reading, so
its details come from quoted search excerpts. Vendors change their screens
often, so check a live demo before copying anything exactly. The links are
at the end.

## In short

1. **Every platform has the same five areas:** a home page of activity, send
   (one-off and automated), tracking, reports, and money (balance or billing).
   Recipients and settings sit around them.
2. **Home is a landing page that starts a send.** Snappy and Sendoso put
   "send" and "automate" at the top. Sendoso changes what's below by role:
   admins see spend, engagement, top campaigns, top senders, pending
   approvals and recipient feedback; a sender sees only their own recent
   activity and how recipients responded.
3. **Tracking is per gift, with actions.** Statuses run from sent through
   opened and chosen to shipped and delivered (or expired). Goody tracks by
   batch, with a recipients tab where a gift can be cancelled.
4. **Reports are filters plus a CSV.** Filter by campaign and date range;
   show sends, acceptance rate, spend, and top products and brands; download
   the raw data.
5. **Money is held, then spent.** Goody holds a gift's full cost from the
   balance until it is accepted, and returns the difference when a recipient
   swaps for something cheaper. PerkUp tops up one company balance and hands
   it out to teams or managers as periodic budgets that don't roll over.
   This matches our ledger (available, held, spent).
6. **Two things recipients value show up on the sender's side:** thank-you
   notes (Snappy has a panel on Home; Sendoso a feedback widget) and
   automated gifts for birthdays, anniversaries and onboarding (all of them,
   usually behind a paid or team plan).
7. **Self-serve exists and is free to start.** Goody's Starter plan is free
   with unlimited seats; paid tiers add international gifts, branding,
   reporting, automation and team budgets. That is the closest model to our
   B2C direction.

## The platforms

### Snappy

- **Menu:** Gift Activity (home), Campaigns, Recipients, Billing, Account
  Settings.
- **Home:**
  - **Send Gifts** and **Automate Gifts** buttons at the top;
  - three suggested shortcuts;
  - browse by gift category;
  - reports further down;
  - a thank-you notes panel on the right.
- **Campaigns:** reusable templates for an occasion (collection, budget,
  branding, messages); drafts can be edited and sent again. Automated
  campaigns (birthdays, anniversaries) take a date per recipient.
- **Tracking:** unopened, opened, unwrapped, claimed, expired. Filters by
  campaign and date range.
- **Reports:** delivery, claims, campaign costs, recipient activity, a
  budget report; downloadable.
- **Money:** a billing method per account (team or department), checked when
  a gift is sent. Leftover funds carry over to future campaigns. Accounts
  inside a company separate teams' spending.

### Sendoso

- **Dashboard (the page after sign-in):**
  - **Admins and managers:** spend and sends for the account, department or
    team over a date range; engagement; top campaigns; top 5 senders;
    pending approvals; recipient feedback with the item and the sender.
  - **Senders:** only their own recent activity and how recipients
    responded.
- **Analytics:** a separate area with tabs for sending and spending.
- **Money:** budgets and funds for teams or individuals (from review
  summaries; the help pages weren't readable).

### Goody (Goody for Business)

- **Pages:** Send, Track, Reports, and a company directory for
  automated gifts.
- **Track:** every gift and batch, with statuses. A batch's Recipients tab
  cancels a gift. For large batches, Reports is the faster way to find links
  and statuses.
- **Reports:** gifts sent and accepted, total spend, acceptance rate, top
  products and brands; a filter for gifts paid from the balance; a raw-data
  download.
- **Money:**
  - pay by card, corporate account or a pre-funded balance;
  - a gift's full cost is held until it is accepted;
  - a cheaper swap returns the difference to the balance;
  - expiry can't be changed on balance-paid gifts.
- **Automation:** pick people in the company directory and turn on gifts
  for birthdays, work anniversaries (a different gift per tenure is
  possible) or onboarding; drafts before switching on. HR systems keep the
  directory in sync.
- **Plans:**
  - **Starter:** free, unlimited seats, US and Canada.
  - **Pro:** per user; international, gift cards, branding, advanced
    reports.
  - **Team:** custom pricing; HR sync, automation, team budgets (one-time,
    monthly, quarterly), Salesforce, SmartLinks.

### PerkUp

- **Roles:** Admin, Manager, Member; each controls who sends, manages
  budgets or sees reports.
- **Money:** one company balance (card or bank transfer). Admins hand it out
  to teams or managers as budgets that reset monthly, yearly or once, and
  don't roll over.
- **Dashboard:** activity, shipment status, redemption rate and spend, with
  downloadable reports.

### Others

Reachdesk, Postal and Loop & Tie appear in every comparison, but their public
pages say little about the dashboard itself:
- **Reachdesk** sells reach (gifting to over 180 countries) and address
  collection;
- **Postal** sells a dashboard for account-based marketing programs;
- **Loop & Tie** sells choice-based, sustainable gifts with engagement data.

Alyce is reported as sunset after Sendoso bought it.

## Where ours stands

The dashboard built on 28 Oct ([plan](../plans/sender-dashboard.md)) follows
the prototype and already covers the common core: a Home with send
shortcuts and the ledger's three numbers, Browse, Campaigns with a funnel, a
send flow, campaign detail and approvals. Holding a gift's cost until it is
chosen is what Goody does too.

**Missing, against the platforms above**, roughly in order of how often
they appear:

1. **Billing and top-up:** every platform has it; ours is a disabled button.
   It depends on the money decision.
2. **Reports with a date filter and CSV download.** Every platform has them;
   ours shows a funnel only. Goody's metrics are a good minimal set: sent,
   accepted, acceptance rate, spend, top products and brands.
3. **Gift actions on the tracking view:** remind, extend, cancel. Already
   listed as out of scope in the dashboard plan.
4. **A recipients page** (contacts, CSV upload), not only a picker in the
   send flow.
5. **Automated gifts** (birthdays, anniversaries, onboarding). Planned for
   M5; the prototype's "Coming up" panel is the home-page entry point.
6. **Thank-you notes**, a low-cost feature that two of the four put on the
   dashboard.
7. **A home that changes by role**, as Sendoso does, if teams and approvers
   stay in the product.

## What this means for B2C

The closest model is Goody's self-serve tier: free to start, one person
sending, paying by card or from a balance, with team features (shared
budgets, approvals, HR sync) as an upgrade. If we follow it:
- the dashboard's company and team become optional, an upgrade rather than
  the default;
- Home keeps the send shortcuts, the balance (or "pay by card") and recent
  activity, without the team switcher;
- approvals and team budgets wait for the team tier.

That is input to the workspace decision, not the decision itself.

## Suggested next steps

1. **Decide the money model** (balance, card per send, or both, as Goody
   does). It unblocks billing, the top-up button and the home page's
   numbers.
2. **Add reports with CSV download** and **gift actions** (remind, extend,
   cancel): the two most common things we lack that don't depend on that
   decision.
3. **Show thank-you notes on Home** when the recipient app (M4) can collect
   them.
